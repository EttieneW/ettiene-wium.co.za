My responsibility

I own planning, estimation, technical decisions, and delivery, including the 2026 domestic-data phase.

Treat Mapper as the client's legacy extractor; own load, schema, validation, and repair on our side so a failed month can be traced through PHP and MySQL instead of guessed.

Failed months get a traced load, schema, and repair path in PHP and MySQL instead of a guessed one. Monthly Mapper sync still runs on tables over two million rows. 2026 domestic-data work is the current annual phase under this lead.
